30 March 2025 · Burgas · Boycho Branzov Hall

Family Sports Half-Day

Rhythmic gymnastics for children, parents and grandparents — movement, smiles and a genuine family experience.

As part of the “One Gram of Life” program, the Hobby Club organized a sports meeting where generations stood side by side. Participants were not merely spectators — they joined in and repeated exercises demonstrated by two major names in Bulgarian rhythmic gymnastics.

Reneta KamberovaWorld all-around champion (2014) and Olympic bronze medalist, Rio 2016.
Simona DyankovaOlympic champion, Tokyo 2020.

The best-performing children, mothers, fathers and grandparents were recognized by the jury — but the most important reward remained the shared time and joy of moving together.

18 May 2025 · Burgas · Adriana Budevska Theatre

Theatre for Children and Parents

With Ivaylo Zahariev — a different journey through imagination, emotions, characters and the stories we create every day.

The theatre becomes a meeting place for children and parents. Through play and participation, families discover how the same situation can be experienced and expressed in different ways.

What happens on stage?

  1. Meeting the actor and discovering the world of theatre.
  2. Imagination, different forms of expression and the power of a message.
  3. How a plot is born — participants create their own story.
  4. What a character is, and why we all experience universal emotions while expressing them in unique ways.
  5. Children and parents take on roles and perform the story they created in their own way.
  6. We look for “plots” and “characters” in everyday life and learn to recognize them.

In the same situation, everyone reacts differently — and theatre helps us see, understand and accept those differences.
