How it all began

“One Gram of Life” began in 2021 not as a finished project, but as a search by parents who wanted to understand how to prepare their children in time.

First, we searched for answers

We began looking for existing prevention programs that could become an additional extracurricular activity for us and our children. Then came the harder question — why, despite existing prevention activities, does the problem persist, and what is missing for prevention to achieve a real result?

We began bringing parents and specialists together

We organized parent meetings, looked for information and asked more and more questions. This led to the First National Conference “One Gram of Life”, where we invited specialists. Not because we already had the answers, but because we had questions and asked for help.

From questions to action

This path showed us the need to develop the first structured module for children and parents, focused on one of the most vulnerable age groups. At the same time, we began work on the film “One Gram of Life” — so that the reality in which our children grow up, and the challenges they will face, could reach many more people.

Today we invite you to stand with us.

If you recognize your own questions, your children and your concerns in this cause — make it your own. “One Gram of Life” is a cause created by parents for children.
