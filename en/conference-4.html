From personal choice to shared change.

The Fourth National Conference focuses on practical models for developing skills, values and responsibility in children — within the family, school and community.

At the Fourth National Conference, we speak not simply about an idea but about a working model — how we build skills, train character through practice, bring prevention into schools and families, and continue developing the mission. An important part of this journey is the film “One Gram of Life”, through which the subject of early prevention reaches a broad audience and becomes a reason for dialogue among children, parents, specialists and communities.

Vision and mission

“One Gram of Life” combines educational and film initiatives, civic engagement and work with families around one common goal — early prevention and the creation of a values-based environment for children and young people.

A working program

The structured model for work with children and parents is presented — a practical approach focused on communication, rules, healthy boundaries, personal responsibility and decision-making skills.

School and family

The focus moves from talking about the problem to everyday practice. Prevention is built through interaction among children, parents, the educational environment, specialists and local communities.

Hobby Clubs

The “One Gram of Life” Hobby Club network develops the model through sport, culture, creativity and shared family experiences — an environment where skills and character are built through participation.

The film “One Gram of Life” — prevention through the power of story

The film is an important part of the national program's development and its approach to prevention. Through human stories and situations close to real life, it opens a space for difficult conversations about drugs, choices, consequences and the role of the family. Screenings become more than cultural events — they become a tool for meeting and dialogue among children and parents, school communities, specialists and citizens. In this way, the message of “One Gram of Life” moves beyond conferences and reaches people directly.
