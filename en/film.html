The story behind the film

Four stories. One school. Consequences that affect everyone.

“One Gram of Life” is based on true incidents and events. The stories follow not only children who begin using drugs, but also their friends, loved ones and families.

A story about choices, pressure from the environment and the conversation that needs to begin in time.

Filming began in May 2025 after extensive preparation, casting and rehearsals.
