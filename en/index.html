How it all began

A parent's concern became a cause.

“One Gram of Life” began in 2021 with parents searching for an answer to one question: How can we prepare our children for risks before they face them?

The search for prevention that truly works led to parent meetings, national conferences, the program for children and parents, and the film “One Gram of Life”.

We began as parents looking for answers. Today we invite you to stand with us.

National program

From information to life skills.

The program works with children in grades 5 and 6 and at the beginning of grade 7 — an age when identity, peer relationships and personal boundaries are taking shape. Across 7 consecutive psychoeducational modules, children and parents build knowledge, confidence and resilient behavior.
